## Hello world!

My first blog post using [Blogatto](https://blogat.to)!

Built with Tailwind, DaisyUI, Lustre and the aforementionned Blogatto, which was just shared by [Veeso](https://github.com/veeso) on the Gleam Discord. After returning from the Gleam Gathering, I feel more motivated than ever to do more stuff with Gleam and Lustre and contribute to the ecosystem. A blog seems like a good place to just dump stuff that could eventually be useful to the community. Either way, the thought did cross my mind of making one, and Blogatto came in at just the right time. Thanks Veeso!
